# Running your site — a plain guide

No tech words. Everything you need to run the site day to day. You do all of
this from **yourdomain.com/admin** after you sign in.

## Signing in

1. Go to **/admin**.
2. Enter your email. You get a link — tap it. No password to remember.
3. If it says the page does not exist, you are not signed in as an admin yet.
   Ask your developer to switch your account to admin once.

---

## 1. Add a new program

1. Go to **Programs → Add a program**.
2. Fill in the name, the one-line description (shows on cards), the full
   description, and the price in pounds.
3. Choose **Live cohort** or **Self-paced**, add the start date and what people
   get.
4. Upload a cover image — any photo you made is fine, the site shrinks it for
   you.
5. Set the status to **Live** when you want it on the site. Leave it on
   **Draft** while you work. **Sold out** shows a "Next cohort soon" badge.

_(The full program editor arrives in Release 2. Until then your developer adds
programs for you, or you edit the seeded ones.)_

## 2. Add a wholesale bundle

1. Go to **Wholesale → Add**.
2. Name it, say what is inside, the number of pieces, your price, and the
   **typical resale value** (this is the number that sells it).
3. Add the stock count. When it reaches zero the bundle shows "Sold out"
   automatically.
4. Upload the photos. The first photo is the one shown on the card.

## 3. Where enquiries arrive

Every time someone uses the contact form, it lands in **Enquiries** — and you
get an email straight away.

- A **red dot** means new. A **hollow dot** means you have read it. A **green
  dot** means you replied.
- Tap a message to open it. From there you can **Reply by email** or **Message
  on WhatsApp** in one tap.
- Add a private note to yourself — only you can see it.

## 4. Mark an order as shipped

1. Go to **Orders** and tap the order.
2. Change its status to **Shipped**.
3. Add the tracking number if you have one — the customer gets an email with it
   automatically.

_(Order fulfilment arrives in Release 2.)_

## 5. Read your analytics

Go to **Analytics**. Pick a time range at the top (7 days, 30 days, and so on).

- The two big numbers are **visitors** and **page views**. Green means up on
  last period, red means down.
- **Where visitors came from** shows TikTok, Instagram, Google and Direct. This
  is the one to watch — it tells you which posts are working.
- The **conversion row** shows how many visitors turned into messages and
  orders.

_(Analytics arrives in Release 3.)_

## 6. Change your details or the homepage

Go to **Settings**. You can change your WhatsApp number, email, booking link
and social handles, and edit the homepage headline and paragraph — no developer
needed. You can also turn on the **announcement bar** to show a message across
the top of the whole site.
